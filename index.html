ABOUT

Linux, the Dovian way.

Dovian GNU/Linux is a Debian-based distribution focused on simplicity, customization, and giving users control over their system.

◆

Debian-based

Built on the Debian ecosystem while adding Dovian's own configuration and tooling.

◇

Open source

The Dovian source code is publicly available on GitHub. Nothing hidden behind a wall.

✦

Customizable

Choose your channel, customize your environment, and make Dovian your own.
